# Demo Messy Startup Vendor Agreement

## Vendor Services And Contractor Agreement

This Vendor Services and Contractor Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of July 1, 2026, by and between **Northstar HealthTech, Inc.**, a Delaware corporation with offices in San Francisco, California ("Company"), and **OpsPilot Solutions LLC**, a single-member consulting company located in Austin, Texas ("Vendor").

Company is an early-stage software startup that provides workflow automation tools to healthcare clinics. Vendor will provide customer onboarding, implementation support, data migration assistance, product operations support, and related services.

This document is intentionally drafted as a demo agreement with multiple legal and business risk issues.

---

## 1. Scope Of Services

Vendor will provide services requested by Company from time to time, including customer onboarding, product configuration, help desk support, training, customer success operations, data migration, internal documentation, quality assurance testing, and any other tasks assigned by Company's Head of Operations.

Vendor will perform the services in accordance with Company's internal policies, support scripts, security procedures, onboarding playbooks, product roadmap priorities, and any instructions provided by Company managers.

Company may change the scope, priority, or timing of Vendor's services at any time based on business needs. Vendor agrees to accept reasonable additional tasks without additional compensation unless Company approves extra fees in writing.

---

## 2. Schedule, Availability, And Work Location

Vendor must be available Monday through Friday from 8:30 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. Pacific Time, excluding Company holidays.

Vendor must attend daily standups, weekly all-hands meetings, product review meetings, customer escalation meetings, and any training sessions required by Company.

Vendor may work remotely, but Vendor must remain logged into Company's Slack, ticketing system, and video conferencing tools during normal business hours.

Vendor must request approval at least five business days before taking time away from the services.

---

## 3. Tools, Systems, And Equipment

Company will provide Vendor with a Company email address, access to internal systems, customer databases, analytics dashboards, support software, and a Company-managed laptop if requested.

Vendor must use Company's approved tools, templates, email signature, customer scripts, and communication style when working with customers.

Vendor may not use alternative tools, subcontractors, or automation systems without Company's prior written approval.

---

## 4. Compensation And Payment

Company will pay Vendor a fixed fee of **$7,500 per month**, payable on Company's regular payroll cycle.

Vendor does not need to submit invoices unless Company requests them.

Company may withhold payment for any month in which Vendor fails to meet Company's performance expectations, customer satisfaction targets, internal response-time standards, or assigned deliverable deadlines.

Expenses will be reimbursed only if pre-approved in writing. Company may reject any expense that it determines is unnecessary or excessive.

No separate compensation will be paid for additional hours, weekend work, emergency customer escalations, or additional services unless approved by Company in writing.

---

## 5. Independent Contractor Classification

The parties agree that Vendor is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent, partner, or joint venturer of Company.

Vendor is responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes, insurance, workers' compensation coverage, licenses, and permits.

Vendor is not eligible for Company health insurance, paid time off, retirement benefits, stock options, bonuses, or other employee benefits.

Vendor agrees that the independent contractor label in this Agreement is conclusive and may not be challenged by Vendor in any agency, court, arbitration, or administrative proceeding.

---

## 6. Exclusivity And Non-Competition

During the term of this Agreement and for twelve months after termination, Vendor may not provide services to any business that offers workflow automation, customer onboarding, healthcare software, data migration, customer success, or product operations services in the United States.

Vendor may not work with any actual or potential competitor of Company without Company's prior written approval.

Vendor may not solicit, accept work from, or communicate with any Company customer, lead, vendor, investor, employee, contractor, or partner for two years after termination.

Vendor acknowledges that these restrictions are reasonable because Company operates in a competitive market and Vendor will receive access to confidential information.

---

## 7. Intellectual Property

All work product, ideas, concepts, inventions, improvements, templates, scripts, processes, workflows, documentation, know-how, software, discoveries, data models, automation logic, prompts, customer communications, and other materials created, conceived, learned, improved, or used by Vendor during the term of this Agreement are the sole property of Company.

Vendor assigns to Company all worldwide rights, title, and interest in all work product and related intellectual property, whether created during working hours or outside working hours, and whether or not related to a specific Company project.

Vendor agrees that any pre-existing materials, tools, templates, scripts, or methods used in performing the services become Company property if they are used in connection with Company's business.

Vendor waives all moral rights, attribution rights, and similar rights to the maximum extent permitted by law.

Vendor must execute any documents requested by Company to confirm or perfect Company's ownership rights.

---

## 8. Confidentiality

Vendor must keep all Company information confidential, including customer information, product plans, pricing, business strategies, investor materials, source code, operational procedures, internal communications, vendor lists, employee information, and the existence and terms of this Agreement.

Vendor may not disclose confidential information to any third party without Company's prior written approval.

Vendor's confidentiality obligations continue indefinitely after termination.

This Agreement does not clearly exclude information that becomes public through no fault of Vendor, information Vendor already knew before disclosure, information independently developed by Vendor, or information required to be disclosed by law.

---

## 9. Data Protection And Security

Vendor may access personal information, customer records, clinic data, support tickets, implementation files, protected health information, login credentials, and other sensitive data.

Vendor must comply with Company's security policies and any privacy, data protection, healthcare, cybersecurity, or artificial intelligence rules Company identifies from time to time.

Vendor must immediately notify Company of any suspected data incident, security event, unauthorized access, lost device, customer complaint, or accidental disclosure.

Vendor is responsible for all losses, fines, penalties, investigation costs, customer notices, remediation costs, and legal fees arising from any data incident connected to Vendor's services, regardless of fault.

This Agreement does not include a separate data processing agreement, HIPAA business associate agreement, security exhibit, audit procedure, breach-notice timeline, data deletion process, or limits on Vendor's access to sensitive data.

---

## 10. Indemnification

Vendor will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Company and its directors, officers, employees, investors, customers, affiliates, agents, successors, and assigns from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, fines, penalties, settlements, costs, and expenses, including attorneys' fees, arising out of or related to:

1. Vendor's services;
2. any act or omission by Vendor;
3. any breach or alleged breach of this Agreement;
4. any customer complaint involving Vendor;
5. any data incident, privacy claim, or security event;
6. any claim that Vendor was misclassified as an independent contractor;
7. any claim by Vendor's personnel, subcontractors, or representatives;
8. any intellectual property claim connected to the services; or
9. Company's use of any work product.

Vendor's indemnity obligations apply even if the claim is caused in part by Company's negligence, instructions, systems, data, or business decisions.

Company may control the defense and settlement of any indemnified claim at Vendor's expense.

---

## 11. Limitation Of Liability

Company will not be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, exemplary, punitive, or lost-profit damages.

Company's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid to Vendor during the one month before the event giving rise to the claim.

Vendor's liability is unlimited.

The liability cap does not apply to Vendor's indemnification obligations, confidentiality obligations, data protection obligations, intellectual property obligations, restrictive covenant obligations, or any claim involving gross negligence, willful misconduct, or violation of law.

---

## 12. Term, Renewal, And Termination

This Agreement begins on July 1, 2026 and continues for an initial term of one year.

The Agreement automatically renews for additional one-year terms unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety days before the end of the then-current term.

Company may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, immediately upon written notice.

Vendor may terminate this Agreement only if Company materially breaches the Agreement and fails to cure the breach within sixty days after written notice.

If Company terminates the Agreement, Vendor must provide transition assistance for up to ninety days at the same monthly fee.

Company may suspend payment during any transition period if Vendor does not cooperate to Company's satisfaction.

---

## 13. Performance Standards

Vendor must meet all performance standards, response-time targets, customer satisfaction targets, quality metrics, and internal deadlines established by Company.

Company may revise the performance standards at any time.

Vendor must correct any work that Company determines is incomplete, late, inaccurate, unclear, or inconsistent with Company expectations.

Failure to meet performance standards may result in non-payment, immediate termination, or both.

---

## 14. Audit And Monitoring

Company may monitor Vendor's communications, system usage, activity logs, work product, customer interactions, time online, response times, and performance metrics.

Vendor must provide records, reports, screenshots, time summaries, customer notes, and other information requested by Company within two business days.

Company may audit Vendor's systems, devices, tools, files, and security practices at any time to verify compliance with this Agreement.

---

## 15. Subcontracting

Vendor may not subcontract, delegate, outsource, or assign any services without Company's prior written approval.

If Company approves a subcontractor, Vendor remains fully responsible for the subcontractor's acts, omissions, taxes, classification, security practices, confidentiality obligations, and compliance with this Agreement.

---

## 16. Employee Handbook And Company Policies

Vendor must comply with Company's employee handbook, code of conduct, anti-harassment policy, remote work policy, expense policy, information security policy, customer communication policy, and any other policy Company provides.

Company may update its policies at any time, and Vendor must comply with updated policies immediately.

---

## 17. Publicity And Customer Communications

Vendor may not identify Company as a customer or use Company's name, logo, screenshots, customer names, or project descriptions in any portfolio, website, resume, social media post, case study, or marketing material without Company's prior written approval.

Vendor may not communicate with Company customers outside approved Company channels.

---

## 18. Governing Law, Venue, And Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict-of-law rules.

Any dispute must be brought exclusively in the state or federal courts located in San Francisco County, California.

Vendor waives any objection to personal jurisdiction, venue, inconvenient forum, or distance from Vendor's residence or place of business.

The parties do not agree to mediation before litigation.

The parties do not agree to arbitration.

The prevailing party in any dispute is entitled to recover attorneys' fees and costs.

---

## 19. Changes To Agreement

Company may modify this Agreement, the scope of services, applicable policies, performance standards, security requirements, payment procedures, and customer communication requirements by providing written notice to Vendor.

Vendor's continued performance after notice constitutes acceptance of the changes.

---

## 20. Return Of Materials

Upon request or termination, Vendor must immediately return or delete all Company materials, confidential information, customer data, credentials, devices, documents, notes, copies, backups, and work product.

Vendor must certify deletion in writing within three business days.

---

## 21. Miscellaneous

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties.

If any provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect.

Company may assign this Agreement in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, asset sale, reorganization, or transfer of any part of its business.

Vendor may not assign this Agreement without Company's prior written approval.

No waiver is effective unless in writing signed by Company.
