# Demo Contractor Agreement

## Independent Contractor Agreement

This Independent Contractor Agreement is entered into between **BrightPath Apps, Inc.** ("Company") and **Jordan Lee** ("Contractor").

## 1. Services

Contractor will provide product support, customer onboarding, and internal operations assistance for the Company.

Contractor will perform tasks assigned by the Company's Head of Operations and will follow the Company's internal policies, procedures, and support scripts.

## 2. Schedule And Work Location

Contractor is expected to be available Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time.

Contractor must attend the Company's daily team standup, weekly staff meeting, and any training sessions required by the Company.

Contractor may work remotely but must remain logged into the Company's communication systems during business hours.

## 3. Tools And Equipment

The Company will provide Contractor with a company email address, access to internal systems, support software, customer databases, and a company laptop if requested.

Contractor must use the Company's approved tools and templates when communicating with customers.

## 4. Compensation

The Company will pay Contractor $5,000 per month, paid on the Company's regular payroll schedule.

Contractor does not need to submit invoices unless requested by the Company.

## 5. Exclusivity

Contractor agrees not to provide similar services to any other software company during the term of this Agreement without the Company's prior written approval.

## 6. Supervision And Performance

The Company may review Contractor's performance, require corrections to Contractor's work, and reassign Contractor to different projects as business needs change.

Contractor must comply with the Company's code of conduct, employee handbook, and security policies.

## 7. Term And Termination

This Agreement begins on June 1, 2026 and continues until terminated.

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days' written notice.

The Company may terminate the Agreement immediately if Contractor fails to follow Company instructions or policies.

## 8. Classification

The parties agree that Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee. Contractor is responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits.

Contractor is not eligible for Company health insurance, paid time off, retirement benefits, or other employee benefits.
